Choosing AI tools for Instagram posts and Reels

Start with the format you actually publish: a caption for a product photo, a sequence of carousel slides or a vertical video. Draft one short brief with the target audience, message and visual style. A writing assistant can help propose caption options; an image tool may help plan graphics; a video editor can shorten footage you already own. Compare the results with the same brief rather than choosing a product from its marketing screenshots.

Useful starting points

Canva Magic Media is listed for creating visuals, ChatGPT can help refine short copy, and Opus Clip is catalogued for turning longer footage into short clips. These are different jobs, so you may need only one of them. The directory cards above show further options from writing, image and video categories.

Check before you post

Review spelling on generated graphics, confirm rights to photos and music, and avoid publishing fabricated testimonials or claims. Check whether a tool exports the aspect ratio and file format you need. AI does not guarantee engagement or follower growth. For broader choices, see tools for content creators and the video guide.
