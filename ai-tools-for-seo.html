AI Tools for SEO

Find relevant AI tools for SEO workflows.

Use AI for SEO without publishing generic pages

Begin with a real search question and decide what the visitor needs to accomplish. Research competing results, check the wording people use and outline the information that would genuinely answer the query. AI tools may help organize notes and draft sections, but they cannot replace checking the underlying facts or demonstrating why your page is useful.

Where AI tools can assist

Frase is listed for content briefs and topic analysis, while Semrush ContentShake is in the catalog for content planning and drafting. A writing assistant such as ChatGPT can help turn confirmed notes into a readable outline. Compare the actual features, export formats and pricing on each provider's site.

How to evaluate the result

Check whether the finished page answers the query directly, cites reliable primary sources when needed and provides examples that are not copied from other websites. Review its title, headings, internal links and mobile readability. Track impressions and clicks in Search Console rather than assuming that an AI-generated score predicts rankings.
