How to make a useful image-generation comparison

Begin with a clear brief: subject, lighting, style, aspect ratio and where the final image will be used. Try the same brief on both services and note the effort needed to improve a small detail. Keep a record of the changes you make instead of judging only the first result. If you need text inside a graphic or recognizable product details, examine those elements closely because generated images can contain errors.

Editing matters as much as generation

Think about what happens after you create an image. Can you refine part of the composition, export at the required size and combine it with your current design files? Does the workflow fit a person making one illustration or a team producing consistent marketing assets? Those questions often matter more than whether a sample gallery looks impressive.

Check access, costs and rights

Plan availability, generation limits and product names change. Verify the current tools and image capabilities directly with the providers before subscribing. For client or commercial projects, read the applicable usage rights and avoid uploading material without permission. For other choices, compare Midjourney alternatives and our image tools guide.
