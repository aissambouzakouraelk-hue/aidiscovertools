Overview

AI Discover Tools is an editorial AI tools website. This privacy policy explains what information may be collected when you browse the site, contact us, or submit a tool for review. We keep this policy focused on practical website usage so visitors can understand how data may be handled in plain language.

Information We May Collect

We may collect basic website analytics such as page views, device type, browser information, and general usage patterns. If you contact us or submit a tool through a form, we may also collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, company name, tool URL, and any message or product description included in the submission.

How We Use Information

We use this information to operate and improve the site, review submissions, respond to messages, identify broken pages, understand which content is most useful, and maintain overall quality. We may also use aggregate usage patterns to improve navigation, editorial priorities, and page structure. We do not publish private contact information submitted through forms unless you explicitly ask us to do so.

Cookies And Advertising

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, site functionality, and advertising services. Third-party providers, including ad platforms, may use cookies to personalize or measure advertising based on visits to this and other websites. If you use browser controls to limit cookies, some features or measurement systems may work differently.

Google tags and Analytics

We use a Google tag with the identifier AW-17735313498 for Google advertising measurement. Google Analytics 4 may also be used to understand aggregated visits, pages viewed, referral sources, devices and site navigation when a connected Analytics property is configured. Google Analytics and AdSense are separate products. We do not use these tools to identify visitors by name.

Google tag measurement starts with consent-related storage denied by default. Where applicable, storage and measurement depend on the visitor's choices through the site's consent mechanism. Please see Google's partner sites information for details about processing.

Optional analytics choices

If the Google advertising consent message is not available, AI Discover Tools may offer a separate choice for optional Google Analytics cookies. You can accept or reject these cookies without losing access to the site. The choice is stored locally on your device so the site can remember it. An "Analytics preferences" control lets you change the decision later. This choice does not give permission for advertising cookies, which are controlled separately.

Google AdSense and advertising choices

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information, including cookies or similar identifiers, to deliver and measure ads, subject to applicable law and visitor choices. Learn more in Google's explanation of data use on partner sites and review advertising preferences at Google My Ad Center.

Where legally required, visitors should receive consent options before advertising identifiers are used. The site's publisher configures its consent management message through Google AdSense Privacy & messaging or another certified provider; the presence of this policy alone does not collect consent. If you do not see a choice interface where expected, contact us.

Affiliate links

Some links to third-party products may be affiliate links if a partnership is active. In those cases, qualifying registrations or purchases may generate a commission for this site. This does not change your contract with the software provider. We do not claim that every outgoing link generates a commission.

Third-Party Links

Many pages on AI Discover Tools link to third-party software websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external services. Before creating an account, making a purchase, or sharing business data with a tool provider, you should review that provider's own privacy policy and terms.

If you have a privacy-related question, want to request correction of submitted information, or need help with a contact or submission record, email us at hello@aidiscovertools.com.
