AI Tools for YouTube

Find relevant AI tools for YouTube workflows.

A realistic YouTube production workflow

Start with a specific viewer question and write the main points before generating a script. Use AI to explore the structure, not to invent evidence or substitute for your own footage. Once the outline is accurate, prepare a shot list and decide whether you need editing, captions, voice-over or thumbnail design. A product that generates an impressive clip may still be a poor fit if it lacks suitable export controls.

Tools for different jobs

ChatGPT can help restructure a script, Descript is catalogued for transcript-based editing, and ElevenLabs is listed for AI voice generation and dubbing. For repurposing an existing long video, compare Opus Clip. Check each provider's current features, policies and commercial-use rights before deciding.

Before uploading a video

Review factual claims, captions, pronunciation and any generated faces or voices. Use media you have permission to publish, and follow the platform's rules on synthetic or altered content. Test a full export before paying for a subscription. The video tools guide and creator guide can help narrow the options.
