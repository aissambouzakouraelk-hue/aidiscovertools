About Alex Morgan

Hi, I'm Alex Morgan, the person behind AI Discover Tools.

I started this site because I was tired of AI tool directories that just list hundreds of products with no real opinion behind them. Every tool featured here is researched in depth — what it actually does well, where it falls short, real pricing, and who it's genuinely worth it for.

My approach is simple: no filler, no inflated claims, and no pretending every tool is perfect. If something isn't worth your money, I'll say so. I look at practical value first: does this tool solve a real problem faster or better than the alternative? Pricing and features are checked regularly and updated when they change.

If you have questions, suggestions, or a tool you think deserves a review, reach out through the Contact page — I read every message.
