Contact AI Discover Tools

If you would like to suggest a new AI tool, report outdated details, or share feedback about the site experience, we welcome your message. AI Discover Tools is built as a curated resource, and community feedback helps us improve listing quality, category organization, and editorial relevance. We review suggestions regularly and use them to identify missing tools, fix broken links, and improve page clarity for users who are comparing options.

When contacting us, it helps to include the exact tool name, official website URL, and a short explanation of why the tool should be listed. If you are reporting an issue, please include the page URL and what looks incorrect. Examples include pricing changes, product rebrands, category mismatches, unavailable links, or duplicated entries. Clear context allows us to review faster and publish updates with better accuracy.

We are especially interested in practical, high-utility tools across writing, image generation, video production, coding, marketing, and productivity workflows. If a tool is relevant for students, freelancers, startups, or business teams, let us know. You can also send suggestions for new guide topics, comparison pages, and audience-specific collections. Our editorial goal is to keep this directory useful, trustworthy, and easy to navigate.

If you are a product team submitting your own platform, include details that help users evaluate fit: pricing model, target audience, core use cases, and a short product summary written in plain language. We prioritize transparent submissions that explain practical value clearly. Promotional claims without context are less likely to be published than submissions that focus on real outcomes and honest limitations.
