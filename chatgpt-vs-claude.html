ChatGPT vs Claude: which fits your work?

Editorial check: 23 November 2026. Pricing tiers reviewed against providers' own pages; limits and availability change.

Both ChatGPT and Claude help with writing, documents and code. Neither is automatically better for every task. The clearest comparison is to give them identical work and assess which answer needs fewer corrections.

For students

Try both assistants on a chapter you already understand. Ask for explanations and practice questions, not a finished assignment. Verify references yourself, especially if your university requires sources or prohibits automated writing.

For work and coding

Give each tool the same email draft, document summary or code bug. Compare not just the first answer but the editing time, unsupported assumptions and accuracy of any citations. For coding, run the proposed tests. Do not upload confidential material without permission.

How to choose without wasting money

  1. Pick three recurring tasks you actually do.
  2. Use identical prompts and files in each product.
  3. Count errors and minutes needed to fix the result.
  4. Try free access before subscribing, and pay only if a specific limitation matters to your workflow.

Current plans

Both products have free and paid access. Prices and features vary by location and can change, so check the official ChatGPT plans and official Claude plans before paying.
